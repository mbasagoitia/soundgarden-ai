# SoundGarden AI Vision

## Overview

SoundGarden AI is an interactive system for generating personalized musical experiences from natural language prompts.

Users describe a mood, scene, memory, environment, or idea in plain language, and the system generates a continuous, original musical soundscape that reflects the described atmosphere.

Examples:

- "A sunrise over the Rocky Mountains"
- "A rainy afternoon in a Parisian cafe"
- "An energetic cyberpunk city at night"
- "A peaceful walk through a wildflower meadow"

The long-term goal is to create a platform that bridges natural language, artificial intelligence, music theory, and audio synthesis.

---

## Long-Term Objectives

### Intelligent Composition

Generate coherent, prompt-relevant musical structures rather than random note sequences. The generated melodies are supplemented with rule-based musical harmonies, form, and textures.

The system should understand:

- Melody
- Harmonic motion
- Rhythm
- Instrumentation

---

### Adaptive Instrument Selection

Automatically select instruments based on prompt semantics.

Examples:

| Prompt Theme | Instrumentation Choice Examples |
|--------------|-----------------------|
| Nature | Piano, strings, flute |
| Sci-fi | Synthesizers, pads |
| Adventure | Brass, percussion |
| Meditation | Ambient textures |

---

### AI-Assisted Musical Immersion

SoundGarden is not a song generator. It is an evolving, interactive soundscape generator that can be used for:

- Focus/deep work
- Exploring musical ideas
- Background ambience
- Meditation
- Creative flow state

---

### Personalized Experience

Long-term versions may adapt generation based on:

- User preferences
- Time of day
- Weather

---

## Technical Vision

Potential future technologies:

- MusicVAE
- Transformer-based music models
- Audio diffusion models
- Vector embeddings for prompt understanding
- Cloud-based rendering services

---

## Success Criteria

A successful SoundGarden experience should make users feel:

"I described a feeling, and the music accurately captured it. The composition evolved naturally and interestingly."
